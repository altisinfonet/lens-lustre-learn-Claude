### 4.1 The `.ts` half: identical by construction

The repair enables JSX mode **only** for `.tsx`/`.jsx`. For the **82 `.ts` files** the repaired
tokenizer executes the original code path unchanged. That they agree is therefore **not evidence** —
it is a tautology, and is recorded as such. The `.ts` half is covered instead by the argument that
there is no mis-lex path in a `.ts` file, with A9-T1/T2 as the demonstration for its two plausible
carriers.

### 4.3 F-29 — the finding the repair actually exposed

> **F-29 (new). The old instrument did not parse the `.tsx` corpus; it text-searched it.**
>
> `A9_coverage_probe.py` measures, for each file, the byte offset at which the tokenizer stopped:
>
> - Old lexer: **824,723 of 892,040 bytes (92.5 %)**; **82 of 107** files fully lexed.
> - Repaired: **892,040 of 892,040 (100.0 %)**; **107 of 107**.
> - Of the 25 `.tsx` files, the old lexer finished **none**. Per-file coverage ran
>   **16.7 % – 51.6 %** (`reengagement-day-50.tsx` 654 / 3,919 B = 16.7 %;
>   `BrandHeader.tsx` 892 / 1,728 B = 51.6 %).
> - **20 of those 25 returned a result anyway**, via the header-complete fallback in

> The fallback is conservative and correct **for the import question**, and it is why the drift
> totals survive. It carries **no information about anything else in those bytes**. Any past or
