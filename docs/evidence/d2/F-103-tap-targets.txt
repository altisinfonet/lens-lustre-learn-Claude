NOTHING INTERSECTS.

The Auditor predicted the three stat tiles WOULD intersect and called it
arithmetic rather than risk. It was arithmetic on an unchecked assumption —
that three tiles side by side are adjacent. They are 28px apart. Recorded
under his name at his instruction, not softened into a caveat: the fifth time
in one day of reasoning about the shape of a thing instead of measuring it.
The check was still right to demand — the same arithmetic left the avatars
23px and Delete 4px.

── THE 4px MARGIN AT DELETE ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

The 4px margin at Delete is a positive margin and not a comfortable one, and if
that row ever tightens it is the first thing to break.

Its neighbours do not grow horizontally: Follow back is already 92.7px wide, so
width:100% holds it and min-width:44px never applies, and the actor names are
inline links with no hit region at all.

AND THOSE 12px ARE DEAD SPACE, which is the sharper reading and it is the
Auditor's. The row container is a plain div with gap-3 and NO onClick. Growing
Delete 8px into that space therefore steals from no neighbour — it converts
space where a tap did NOTHING into space where a tap DELETES, and deletion here
has no undo. It is the only change tonight that makes a mis-tap worse rather
than better.

DELETE STAYS AT 44 x 44. The Auditor's ruling, his risk appetite, recorded as
his: the Owner is blind and works from a phone, so the cost of a target he
cannot reliably reach is paid EVERY time he uses the page while the cost of a
mis-tap is paid occasionally, and trading a certain daily difficulty for a
possible occasional one is the wrong direction. 44 is the figure WCAG 2.5.5 and
Apple's guidance both give; 32 is only this project's floor for the SHORT side,
a weaker standard and not one to park a destructive control on. 4px versus 10px
does not change the class of the risk, only the number.

THE CORRECT MITIGATION FOR A NO-UNDO ACTION IS AN UNDO, NOT A SMALLER TARGET.
Recorded as F-106 — a toast with an Undo action and a short window, deletion
deferred or reversible for that window, following what the Owner asked us to
match. NOT in #196, and the Auditor takes it to him; it is not D2's to start.

── THE ACTOR NAMES HAVE NO HIT REGION, AND THAT IS DELIBERATE ────────────────

The inline member names in a notification sentence are links with NO enlarged
hit region, and that is correct rather than an oversight. A name inside a
sentence is not a 44px control, and giving it one would swallow the sentence
around it — the 44px box would extend above and below the line and overlap the
words on the neighbouring lines. Written here explicitly so the next reader does
not file it as a miss.
