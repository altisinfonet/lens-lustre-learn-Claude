That is the whole idea. Nothing is deleted and nothing is hidden — tapping any square opens the full photograph with its caption, reactions and comments exactly as now. The scrolling feed stays, as the second tab.

  • Why it matters for new sign-ups: someone deciding whether to follow a photographer judges the body of work, not one picture. Today they must scroll nine times to see nine photographs. In a grid they see nine at once, and a strong portfolio reads as strong immediately.
  • Why grid can be the default here: measured on your database — all 210 posts carry a photograph, and none are text-only. On a platform where every post is a picture, the frame around each one (name, time, caption, buttons) is using most of the screen.
  • The small marker in a corner appears on the 24 posts that hold more than one photograph, so a set is not mistaken for a single frame.
