Guideline 5.1.2(i): this version removes all third-party analytics and advertising scripts from the iOS app, and removes the cookie prompt. The app does not track users and does not collect cookies for tracking, so it does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. The app only stores what it needs to keep a member signed in.

Guideline 2.3.6: the app has an age check and no parental controls. Members must be 18 or over.
Where to find it: the demo account has already passed this step, so please create a new account. After the new account is confirmed, the "Let's set up your profile" screen appears. Date of Birth is required, and the screen says "18+ only. Under 18 not accepted." Choosing a date under 18 shows "Under 18 is not eligible to register." and sign-up cannot be finished.
Our Terms of Service (https://www.50mmretina.com/page/terms-of-service), section 8 "Minimum Age Requirement and Age Verification", states the same strict 18+ rule.
Members who have already signed up, such as the demo account, see "18+ Only Restricted" beside Date of Birth in Edit Profile.
